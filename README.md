## About me

I'm a technology entrepreneur and the founder of **GlowEra**, working at the intersection of beauty, technology and business. I'm passionate about innovation, artificial intelligence and building digital products that solve meaningful problems.

## Currently building — GlowEra

GlowEra is an early-stage BeautyTech startup focused on making technology more accessible to beauty and wellness businesses, starting in Paraguay.

Our vision is to connect people with beauty and wellness professionals while helping businesses simplify their day-to-day operations.

**Current stage:** Product development and market validation.
